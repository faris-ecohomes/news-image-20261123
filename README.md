# news-image-20261123 — Monday 23 November 2026 in one picture

A newspaper front page that sums up Monday 23 November 2026 in a single
1024×1024 image: 36 stories, all verified from named sources and dated to
that day.

## What the picture shows

- **3 hero panels** with scene illustrations — Ukrainian naval drones sink
  the Russian corvette Merkury off Benghazi; Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang
  and markets erupt; Russian strikes kill 14 in the Kyiv region.
- **11 more panels**: the COP31 fallout, Iran defying the US blockade of
  Hormuz, CrowdStrike's $43.9bn Zscaler deal, the Democrats' 258-seat House
  majority, the first state suits over Grok, Indonesia's factory fire,
  Belgium's general strike, the Modi–Nepal thaw, the Megaspeed blacklist,
  Manchester's arena scare and FIFA's Women's World Cup hosts.
- **"The day in numbers"** — six big statistics of the day.
- **A UTC timeline** of the day as reported, from wire timestamps.
- **"Where it happened"** — a dotted world map with 14 numbered pins.
- **"Also on Monday"** — 22 more verified stories, numbered 15–36.
- **A DATE CHECK box** listing the big stories deliberately left out
  because they broke before Monday (Huang's resignation, the Benghazi
  attack itself, the COP31 collapse, the Delta outage, the Sunset Limited
  derailment) and the ones still to come (Korea's rate decision,
  Arsenal v Dortmund, Spain's 29-N vote).

Every story was cross-checked against its wire dateline, not an
aggregator timestamp.

## How it runs

`main.py` copies the checked-in `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`
after verifying its PNG signature and 1024×1024 size — no network, no
rendering, byte-identical on every run. `gen_poster.py` + `stories.py`
are the build tools that drew the poster during the build session.
